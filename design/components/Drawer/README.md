The drawer is the job detail sheet: a right-anchored `bg` panel at `size-drawer-max` (768px) with a `line` left edge and `shadow-drawer` (a stacked sheet to its left), over the `backdrop`, padded `space-5` (`space-6` at md) with `space-5` between sections.

Its order is fixed: title in `sheet-title` with the id in `mono` and a close `.btn.icon`; a chip row (stage `ink`, `StateChip`, round `muted`, `PrChip`, kind `info`); the job's daemon session activity in `caption` (the card's line: `LiveDot` and *working · 4s ago*, the `warn` chip *needs permission*, or *idle* / *waiting*; absent with no session); the reason; the progress bar; a fact grid (three columns of `label` over `mono` values, links in `ink`); `DepSection`; the verdict matrix and usage tables; the budget sheet; screenshots; then the Files / Timeline / Thread tabs. Timeline rows that arrived since the drawer opened, and Thread messages that are new or changed since, start with a 6px `dot hl` and a visually hidden *new*; reopening resets that. The Thread tab carries a `chip hl` *N new* while the job's Thread has requests, replies or status changes you have not had on screen (kept per job for the page's lifetime; a job's first load counts as read). Its times, and the Created and Updated facts, tick every second. The body scrolls under a bottom action dock (`border-t` `line`, `bg`, capped at 45dvh) that holds *Your move* (permission prompts, Resume, the gate's `JobActions`) on every tab and is absent when nothing needs a person. Escape and the backdrop close it.
