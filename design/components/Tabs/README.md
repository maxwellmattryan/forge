Tabs are `nav`-style labels on a `line` baseline; the active tab draws its own three sides in `line` and fills with opaque `panel`, so it reads as a folder tab cut into the rule. Inactive tabs are `muted`; on hover they turn `fg` and sketch: 1px `sketch-line` lines at 45° fade in and slide into place over 160 ms. The active tab never sketches.

Use tabs to switch facets of one object (the drawer's Thread, Log, Files, Timeline, Details); counts beside a label are `muted`. Many sections get the settings rail instead.

Static rendition in the Drafting direction; replaces the drawer's tab strip.
