Twenty-three line icons drawn for the Drafting direction, one file each: a 24×24 viewBox, 1.25px stroke (`stroke-icon`), round caps and joins, no fills, and on most glyphs a piece of construction geometry (a dashed pitch circle on the cog, a centre line under the chart, a construction circle around the close and plus marks) drawn dashed 2/2 at 60% opacity.

In the app they render inline (`Icon`) with `stroke="currentColor"`, so they take the text colour: `muted` on an inactive nav item, `fg` when active, `ink` on a live control. These files are inked in vellum `ink` (`#1f4e8c`) because an `<img>` cannot inherit colour; for a cyanotype ground, inline the path. The `general` icon's knobs are open in the files and masked with the ground colour inline.

Names and uses: `pipeline`, `inbox`, `backlog`, `agents`, `analytics`, `evals` label the six views; `general`, `pipeline`, `review`, `agents`, `tracker`, `dashboard`, `daemon`, `diagnostics`, `about` label the nine settings tabs; `close`, `command`, `search`, `play`, `stop`, `refresh`, `copy`, `settings` are controls; `board` and `table` label the layouts. Draw a new icon as one path on the same grid at the same stroke, and give it one construction mark.
