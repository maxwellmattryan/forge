# Accessibility lens (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Check:
- Semantic elements and landmarks; headings in order; one h1 per view.
- Every interactive element is reachable and operable by keyboard with a visible focus indicator.
- Accessible names for icon buttons, inputs, images (meaningful alt or alt="").
- Colour contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for UI components; information not conveyed by colour alone.
- Dialogs trap and restore focus; live regions announce async results.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; targets are at least 24×24 px.

Save screenshots of what you checked to the job's `screenshots` dir (in your brief) as `<lens>-<what>.png` and list them under Verified; if you could not capture any, say so.
