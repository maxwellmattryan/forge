A button is a forged slug: a 1px `line-strong` seam, cut `radius-nick` (5px) at the top-left and bottom-right corners, `shadow-bevel` inside it, the `control` style (DM Mono 12px, uppercase, .06em) in `fg`, `space-3`/8px inset, filled with the surface it sits on. `.primary` fills with the brushed `--gleam-fill` gradient and its specular top line, sets `on-gleam` text and sits on `shadow-primary`, a solid 2px `gleam-deep` lip: the edge of the steel turned away from the light.

Use `.btn` for every action that is not the surface's one primary action (filters, Start/Stop, Link, Close, toggles) and `.primary` once per surface (Run next, Approve, Save config). Flush a group of related buttons into a `.seg` strip (they share seams; only the strip's outer corners keep their nicks). An icon-only button is `.btn.icon` with an `aria-label`; on phones add `.touch` for a 44px target. `.ghost` drops the seam and bevel at rest for the status bar's indicators and the quiet close.

Hover lights the face: the seam turns `gleam` and the fill takes a 12% gleam tint, over 120 ms, only where the pointer can hover, and `muted` text inside takes the button's `fg`. A primary button goes `gleam-hot` and glows (`shadow-glow`), like steel catching the light. Focus is a solid 2px `line-strong` outline at 2px. Pressed sinks 1px into `raised`; a primary button drops onto its lip (translate 2px, no shadow). Disabled is 40% with a dashed seam and no bevel. A selected toggle (`.sel`) is the same brushed gleam with a 2px `gleam-deep` bottom edge. Never round a button: its corners are cut or square. Never blur its lip.

Static rendition in the Smithy direction, hand-written from `design/components/Button/preview.html` and `dashboard/style.css (@utility btn)`.
