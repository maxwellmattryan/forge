A card is a plate: an opaque `panel` fill with the fine film grain over it, inside a 1px `line` seam, cut `radius-cut` (10px) at the top-left and bottom-right corners and chamfered `radius-1` at the other two, with `shadow-bevel` inside it (a 1px `edge-light` highlight along the top edge, a 1px `edge-dark` shadow along the bottom), so it reads as a trimmed sheet of iron rather than a drawn box. The grain sits on the plate's own clipped `::after` layer and the plate clips its overflow to the cut, so nothing ever paints outside the corner. `.flat` drops the grain where lines are dense (table wrappers, the log pane). Padding is `space-3` for a dense card, `space-4` for a standard one.

Four plates mean four things: a plain plate is a thing (a job, a message, a KPI); an open plate (`.open`, a `line-soft` seam and no fill) is a container (a board column); an ember plate (`.ember`, an `ember` seam plus a 3px `ember` bar down its left edge) is a thing that waits on a human; a riveted plate (`.riveted`) is a plaque: square-cornered, no cut, bolted down with a slotted domed rivet in a dark countersink at each corner, 10px in from both edges; the brand plaque and the page header only. A clickable plate is a `<button>`; hover hardens the seam to `line-strong` at once, with no sheen and no glow, while the fill stays `panel`; focus is the solid 2px ring. An open container stays transparent, drawn on the ground; the plates inside it are opaque.

Plates never nest more than one level, never fill with colour, never cast a shadow except the overlay's and drawer's. Titles inside are `card-title` or `section-title`; counts beside them are `label` in `muted`.

Static rendition in the Smithy direction, hand-written from `design/components/Card/preview.html` and `dashboard/style.css (@utility card)`.
