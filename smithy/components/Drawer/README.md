The drawer is the job detail plate: a right-anchored opaque `panel` plate (`.solid`) at `size-drawer-max` (768px) with a `line` left seam and `shadow-drawer`, over the `backdrop`, padded `space-5` (`space-6` at md) with `space-5` between sections.

Above the tabs it keeps only a compact header, in a fixed order: title in `sheet-title` with the id in `mono`, *Command for this job* and a close `.btn.icon.ghost`; one wrapping summary row (stage `gleam`, `StateChip`, round `muted`, `PrChip`, kind `info`, then the job's daemon session activity in `caption`: the card's line, `LiveDot` and *working · 4s ago*, the `warn` chip *needs permission*, or *idle* / *waiting*; absent with no session); the reason on one truncated line with the full text in its title. Then the tabs, Thread / Log / Files / Timeline / Details: an ARIA tablist (arrows, Home and End move and select) sticky and opaque at the top of the scrolling body, scrolling sideways when the tabs outgrow a phone. Opening a job lands on Thread at the top; switching tabs past the header lands the new tab under the stuck strip. Log holds the session feed alone, and its label shows the `LiveDot` while the session works. Details holds the full reason, the progress bar, a fact grid (three columns of `label` over `mono` values, links in `gleam`), `DepSection`, the verdict matrix, the budget plate, the usage table and screenshots. Timeline rows that arrived since the drawer opened, and Thread messages that are new or changed since, start with a 6px `dot spark` and a visually hidden *new*; reopening resets that. The Thread tab carries a `chip spark` *N new* while the job's Thread has requests, replies or status changes you have not had on screen (kept per job for the page's lifetime; a job's first load counts as read). Its times, and the Created and Updated facts, tick every second. The body scrolls under a bottom action dock (`border-t` `line`, `panel`, capped at 45dvh) that holds *Your move* (permission prompts, Resume, the gate's `JobActions`) on every tab and is absent when nothing needs a person. Escape and the backdrop close it.

Static rendition in the Smithy direction, hand-written from `design/components/Drawer/preview.html` and `components/Drawer.svelte`.
