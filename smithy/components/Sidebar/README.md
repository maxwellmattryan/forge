The sidebar is the anvil: a `size-sidebar` (236px) column of opaque `chrome`, the darkest surface in Forge and the lightest in Ash, with a `line-strong` right edge and `shadow-chrome` over the main column (the sidebar sits at z-10; the drawer and dialogs still cover it), so the grain stops at its edge. Top to bottom it holds the brand plaque (a riveted plate: a square-cornered plate bolted onto the chrome at its four corners, carrying the mark, *Forge* in `sheet-title` and *software factory* in `label`, like a maker's plaque on a machine), a wide stamp line naming the view (`PIPELINE`), the repo in `mono`, the *New job or command* input, the view nav, and at the foot the Settings item. Nothing else: the version, the API, the sessions and the sheet number live in the status bar's facts strip.

The input is a real `input` at full sidebar width with a `line-strong` seam, its placeholder in `muted` (the only visible label) and the `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` hint drawn inside it in `muted` mono. Typing or pasting there opens the command palette with the text, and the input clears; Enter or ArrowDown in the empty input opens it empty; ⌘K focuses the input and a second ⌘K opens the palette. It is the only element with `aria-keyshortcuts`. It sits above the nav because a job starts here more often than a view changes.

Use it as the one navigation surface at md and up; below md the items become a horizontal strip, the settings button a 44px target, and the input a 44px `btn` with the `command` glyph and *New job or command* in sentence case between the nav and the settings button. It holds navigation and the one entry point: the command palette also opens from the drawer (see CommandBar), and the live sessions heartbeat is in the status bar (see StatusBar and LiveDot).
