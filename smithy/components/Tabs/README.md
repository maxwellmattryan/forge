Tabs are `control`-style labels on a `line` baseline; the active tab draws a 2px `gleam` baseline over the rule and goes `fg`. Inactive tabs are `muted`; on hover they turn `fg` and warm (a 10% gleam tint of their surface). The active tab never takes the sheen tint.

Use tabs to switch facets of one object (the drawer's Thread, Log, Files, Timeline, Details); counts beside a label are `muted`, in parentheses: *Timeline (14)*. Many sections get the settings rail instead.

Static rendition in the Smithy direction, hand-written from `design/components/Tabs/preview.html` and `components/Drawer.svelte`.
